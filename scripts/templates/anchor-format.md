# LMPYOG Script Template — Anchor Format
**Version:** 1.0 | **Target runtime:** 9–12 min | **Words/min at delivery pace:** ~140

---

## PRE-PRODUCTION CHECKLIST

Before you write a single word:
- [ ] Primary source confirmed and saved to `research/primary-docs/`
- [ ] All 3 receipts identified with source URLs
- [ ] Story cleared the do/don't list in CLAUDE.md
- [ ] Title candidate written before the script (title shapes the hook)

## ═══════════════════════════════════════
## SECTION 1: HOOK
**Timestamp:** 0:00–0:15
**Word budget:** Under 15 words
**Rule:** Lead with the most insane *verifiable* fact. No setup. No "hey guys." Hit.
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

```
[HOOK LINE — ONE SENTENCE, MAX 15 WORDS]
[HOOK LINE]:

```

**Guidance:** The hook is a claim that makes a reasonable person say "wait, what?" before they can click away. It should be provable — you're going to prove it in the PROOF section. Don't editorialize here. Just drop the fact.

**Hook tests:**
- Would this stop a scroll?
- Is it verifiable?
- Does it set up the rest of the video?

## ═══════════════════════════════════════
## SECTION 2: STAKES
**Timestamp:** 0:15–0:45
**Word budget:** ~120 words
**Rule:** Answer "why should I care" before the viewer can think to ask.
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

```
[STAKES PARAGRAPH — 3–4 sentences. Connect the story to the viewer's life, money, rights, or reality.]

"Here's what this actually means for you..."
[VIEWER RELEVANCE LINE]

"And here's why they don't want you to know..."
[MOTIVE LINE — institution, political actor, or financial interest]

"So let me show you the receipts."
[TRANSITION TO PROOF]
```

## ═══════════════════════════════════════
## SECTION 4: PUNCHLINE
**Timestamp:** 6:00–9:00
**Word budget:** ~400 words
**Rule:** Sarcastic synthesis. You've shown the receipts — now *conclude*. This is where your voice lives.
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

```
[SYNTHESIS — connect all 3 receipts into a single thesis sentence]

"So let me get this straight..."
[THE RECAP — restated sarcastically, building to absurdity]

[THE OBSERVATION — the thing that should be obvious to anyone paying attention]

[THE PUNCHLINE — single devastating sentence. This is the clip.]

[BROADER IMPLICATION — zoom out. What does this mean for the pattern, not just this story?]
```

**Punchline criteria:**
- Does it land without the surrounding context? (Clip test)
- Does it contain a verifiable claim, not just an opinion?
- Would it make someone share it or argue with it? (Both are fine)

## ═══════════════════════════════════════
## DEMONETIZATION RISK CHECKLIST
*Complete before submitting final draft*
## ═══════════════════════════════════════

Run through every line and check:

- [ ] **Violence** — No glorification, incitement, or graphic descriptions of harm
- [ ] **Medical** — Zero health, treatment, or medical efficacy claims
- [ ] **Election integrity** — Every claim has a named primary source document
- [ ] **Targets** — All named individuals are public figures in their public capacity
- [ ] **Speculation** — Any inference is labeled as such ("this suggests," "it appears")
- [ ] **Profanity** — Under YouTube's threshold for ad-friendly (flag any f-bombs or equivalents)
- [ ] **Hate speech** — No slurs, no dehumanizing language
- [ ] **Sensationalism** — Does the thumbnail/title match actual content? (YT penalizes mismatch)
- [ ] **Legal** — Any legal claims cite case number + jurisdiction

**Risk level assigned by reviewer:**
- 🟢 GREEN — Ad-friendly, no flags
- 🟡 YELLOW — Minor flags, suggest edits before upload
- 🔴 RED — Hold for revision, significant demonetization or legal risk
