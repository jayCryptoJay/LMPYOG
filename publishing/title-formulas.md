# LMPYOG Title Formulas

## The Master Formula

```
[SPECIFICITY ANCHOR] + [ACTION/REVELATION] + [STAKES SIGNAL]
```

The specificity anchor is what makes people believe you have the goods — a dollar amount, a named entity, a document, a date. The action/revelation is what happened. The stakes signal tells them why they should care.

---

## Formula Library

### Formula 1: The Dollar Amount Drop
```
The $[AMOUNT] [ENTITY] [ACTION] Nobody Reported
```
Examples:
- `The $40M Dark Money Trail They Buried on Page 2`
- `The $2.7 Billion Contract Nobody Asked About`
- `This $800K FARA Filing Changes Everything`

**Best for:** Follow the Money pillar
**Why it works:** Numbers create specificity. Specificity creates credibility. Credibility earns the click.

---

### Formula 2: The Caught Lacking Frame
```
[PERSON/INSTITUTION] Said [X]. Here's the [DOCUMENT/CLIP/RECORD].
```
Examples:
- `They Said It Was Safe. Here's the Inspector General Report.`
- `He Called It a Conspiracy. Here's His Own Signature.`
- `The Agency Denied It. Here Are the Emails.`

**Best for:** Caught Lacking pillar
**Why it works:** The structure implies confrontation without saying "gotcha." The receipts do the talking.

---

### Formula 3: The Buried Story
```
[MAJOR CLAIM] — [WHY YOU MISSED IT]
```
Examples:
- `Foreign Agents Running This Lobbying Firm — Your News Didn't Cover It`
- `This Senate Vote Changed Your Healthcare — You Heard Nothing About It`
- `The Nonprofit That Got $200M in Contracts Nobody Talked About`

**Best for:** Didn't Tell You pillar
**Why it works:** Implies the viewer was denied information — activates the "I deserve to know this" instinct.

---

### Formula 4: The Document Reveal
```
I Read the [DOCUMENT TYPE]. Here's What They Don't Want You To See.
```
Examples:
- `I Read the FARA Filing. Here's What They Don't Want You To See.`
- `I Read All 400 Pages of the IG Report. Here's Page 312.`
- `This 990 Form Is Being Ignored. I Don't Know Why.`

**Best for:** Receipts pillar
**Why it works:** Positions the creator as the person who did the work so the viewer doesn't have to. Trust builder.

---

### Formula 5: The Rapid Fire Setup
```
[NUMBER] Stories Your News Skipped This Week
```
Examples:
- `5 Stories They Killed This Week (And Why)`
- `3 Things That Happened While You Were Watching the Circus`
- `What Actually Happened This Week (Nobody's Talking About This)`

**Best for:** Rapid Fire pillar
**Why it works:** Listicle format signals density + efficiency. Appeals to viewers who feel overwhelmed.

---

### Formula 6: The Pattern Exposure
```
This Keeps Happening. Nobody's Connecting the Dots.
```
Examples:
- `The Same Lobbying Firm. The Same Vote. Every Time.`
- `Four Senators. Four Votes. One Donor. Nobody's Connecting the Dots.`
- `This Pattern Goes Back 20 Years. Here's the Timeline.`

**Best for:** Any pillar when you have a systemic story
**Why it works:** Implies exclusive insight. "Nobody else is doing this work" framing without saying it.

## Anti-Patterns (What Not To Do)

| Bad title pattern | Why it fails | Better alternative |
|---|---|---|
| `You WON'T BELIEVE What [X] Did` | Pure clickbait, no specificity | `What [X] Did Is in the Public Record. Here It Is.` |
| `MASSIVE Scandal EXPOSED!!!` | Vague + desperate | `The [Specific Scandal] Nobody Named` |
| `[Person] Is a LIAR` | Legal risk + YouTube flags | `[Person] Said [X]. The Document Says [Y].` |
| `The TRUTH About [Topic]` | Overused, zero information | `The [Topic] Document They Didn't Show You` |
| `[Person] DESTROYS [Other Person]` | Debate framing, not your brand | Keep the focus on receipts, not combat |

## Title Writing Checklist

Before locking a title:
- [ ] Contains at least one specific element (number, name, dollar amount, document type)
- [ ] Does not make a claim the video doesn't prove
- [ ] Under 70 characters (for search display)
- [ ] Passes the "would I click this at 2am" test
- [ ] Does not use the word "EXPOSED," "DESTROYED," or "BOMBSHELL" unless that's genuinely ironic
- [ ] Does not name private individuals
- [ ] Has a companion thumbnail concept that matches (title-thumbnail mismatch kills retention)
